What we do

01

Television Curation

Selection & Sizing

Our craft

Founded by two broadcast engineers who missed good pictures

Halden Screenworks began in a Bristol workshop in 2014, after founders Nora Halden and Elias Rowe grew tired of seeing superb panels ruined by bright walls, wrong sizes and factory settings. A decade on, our approach hasn't changed: measure the room first, choose the screen second, and tune everything by eye and instrument on the day.

Principle

The room leads

Lighting, wall colour and seating decide the specification. We design around your home rather than asking your home to work around a television.

Principle

Honest guidance

If a smaller, simpler screen is the right answer, that's what we recommend. Our quotes itemise everything, and we explain why each element is there.

Principle

Finished means finished

Cables hidden, settings labelled, remotes paired, household shown how it works. A job isn't signed off until everyone in the home is comfortable with it.

How it works

From first call to film night

Step 01

Room consultation

A free 45-minute visit or video call. We photograph the space, note the light through the day, and talk about how you actually watch — sport, films, gaming, background news.

Step 02

Written proposal

Within three working days you receive a shortlist of screens, mount options and an itemised quote. No pressure, no expiry countdown — take the time you need.

Step 03

Installation day

Our two-person team fits, calibrates, conceals and demonstrates. Most homes are finished in a single visit, and we return after two weeks for a free follow-up check.
